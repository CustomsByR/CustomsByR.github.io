From photo to rug

Every piece starts with something real — a picture, a story, a face. It's sketched, punched by hand with a tufting gun, then trimmed, sealed and finished.

  • 1Send a photo. A portrait, a pet, or an idea you want reworked into thread.
  • 2We sketch it. You approve the design and size before any yarn is touched.
  • 3Hand-tufted. Punched, sheared and finished by hand — usually 2–3 weeks.
  • 4Shipped to you. Rolled, packed and sent worldwide.
